Our programs

How we create change in the field.

  1. 01

    Community financial camps

    Held in housing societies, community centres and workplaces — safe spaces where domestic workers, maintenance staff and service providers engage with financial systems on their own terms.

  2. 02

    Financial literacy workshops

    Interactive sessions designed for diverse backgrounds, using case studies and practical exercises to foster a culture of saving and investment.

  3. 03

    Mentorship program

    Participants are paired with trained advisors who support them throughout their financial journey, addressing unique challenges on the way to independence.

  4. 04

    Government scheme navigation

    Clear pathways to vital benefits including health insurance and social security programs, turning bureaucratic complexity into accessible opportunity.

  5. 05

    Digital banking initiatives

    Bridging the digital divide so community members can navigate online banking platforms and mobile payment apps with confidence.

A comprehensive, multilingual financial awareness session for community helpers — housekeeping staff, security personnel, landscaping workers, drivers, plumbers, electricians and domestic helps.

  • Facilitated 550 registrations for Ayushman Bharat Health Insurance

  • Established a follow-up system for continued support

Ongoing community support

Zero-balance account setup, at people’s doorsteps.

Beyond structured camps, our coordinators stay present in communities and help people access financial services with minimal barriers.

  • Home visits for documentation
  • Complete paperwork, removing literacy barriers
  • Coordinate with banking partners
  • Ensure quick account activation
  • Hand-deliver materials to residents

This service has facilitated 50+ additional bank accounts — eliminating barriers like time away from work, transport costs and complex paperwork.
