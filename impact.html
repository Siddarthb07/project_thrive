Our Programs

How we create change in the field.

  • Community Financial Camps — full-day experiences pairing teaching and desks.
  • Financial Literacy Workshops — modular sessions for Resident Welfare Associations and employers.
  • Mentorship Program — peer volunteers guiding scheme paperwork and digital safety.
  • Government Scheme Navigation — Jan Dhan, APY, insurance schemes explained in Kannada, Hindi, and English.
  • Digital Banking Initiatives — UPI hygiene, phishing awareness, branch etiquette.
