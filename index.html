Our story

One question revealed a much wider gap.

Aarin, the founder of Project Thrive, noticed his mother paying their housemaid’s salary in cash. When he asked why, she explained that the housemaid did not have a bank account.

It highlighted the reality faced by many domestic workers in India who have never stepped into a bank, used an ATM, or transacted with a bank account. Project Thrive was founded to bridge that gap.
