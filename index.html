Success story

From Uncertainty to Financial Security

Before Project Thrive’s camp at APR Bengaluru, I had never set foot in a bank. The concept of savings, insurance, or pension plans felt like a distant reality meant for others.

Today, she has a savings account, health insurance for her family, and is actively contributing to a pension scheme. Her children’s education is secured, and for the first time, she feels prepared for emergencies.

This transformation happened in just one afternoon—but its impact will last a lifetime.

Our Story

Founded with Purpose

Founded by Aarin Pathak, Project Thrive recognizes that financial exclusion perpetuates inequality. Since 2024, we’ve worked to dismantle barriers that keep low-income families out of mainstream financial systems—government programs, savings, digital payments, and responsible money habits.
